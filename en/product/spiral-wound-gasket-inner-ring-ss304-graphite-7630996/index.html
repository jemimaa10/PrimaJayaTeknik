Spiral Wound Gasket with Inner Ring
Features:

A Spiral Wound Gasket with Inner Ring is equipped with a solid metal inner ring that acts as a compression stop and fills the annular space between the flange bore and the gasket's inside diameter.

The inner ring is designed to prevent the accumulation of solid particles, reduce process fluid turbulence, and minimize erosion of the flange faces. It is ideal for use with male and female pipe flanges and is suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature applications.
